Community Guidelines

Last updated: 13 October 2026

FION is a place to learn and to meet other learners. Be kind and keep it safe for everyone, including teenagers.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or discrimination.
  • Sexual or pornographic content, nudity, and any sexual content involving minors (reported to the authorities).
  • Graphic violence, self-harm promotion, dangerous challenges, or instructions for causing harm.
  • Scams, phishing, fake giveaways, malware, spam and unwanted promotion.
  • Impersonating other people, doxxing or sharing other people's private information.
  • Illegal content, or content that infringes copyright or other rights.

What you can do

  • Report a profile, Reel, post, comment or message: open the ⋯ menu (or the flag on a message or comment) and choose Report.
  • Block a user from their profile, the chat header or the Reel menu. Blocked users cannot message or call you and you will not see their content. Manage the list in Settings → Blocked users.

What we do

We review reports and remove content that breaks these rules, normally within 24 hours. Accounts that repeatedly or seriously break the rules are suspended or permanently banned.
