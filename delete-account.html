Delete your FION account

Last updated: 13 October 2026

You can delete your FION account and the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open FION and sign in.
  2. Open the menu (☰) → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, type the confirmation word and, if you use email sign-in, your password.
  4. Tap Delete permanently. Everything is removed right away.

Option 2: without the app

If you cannot sign in, message us in Telegram @whxxxk from where you can prove the account is yours (the email or username of the account). We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Profile, username, bio, photo and banner.
  • Follows, blocks, posts, Reels, stories and their comments.
  • Your chat messages and files, support requests, "last seen" and push-notification token.
  • Learning progress and the sign-in record in Firebase Authentication.

What may remain

  • Video files of deleted Reels may stay in storage for up to 30 days before they are purged.
  • Likes and comments you left on other people's content may remain without a profile attached.
  • Reports about abuse may be kept where needed for safety or legal reasons.
