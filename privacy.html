Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

FION ("we", "us") is an educational app and website for learning programming, with profiles, chat, photo posts, Reels (short videos) and calls between users. This policy explains what data we process and why. It applies to the FION website and the FION Android app.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and sign-in data (email + password, or Google sign-in), display name, unique username, optional bio, profile photo and banner.
  • Content you create: chat messages and files you send, photo posts, Reels (videos), stories, comments, likes, follows, and reports you submit.
  • Learning data: lessons completed, XP, hearts and settings (stored on your device and, when signed in, in your account).
  • Activity data: "last seen" time, a push-notification token (if you allow notifications), and the people you block.
  • AI assistant: the questions you type are sent to a Google Gemini model through Google Firebase AI Logic to produce an answer.
  • Calls: voice and video calls run directly between devices (WebRTC). Audio and video are not recorded or stored by us. Relay servers (TURN) may carry the encrypted media when a direct connection is not possible. The app asks for camera and microphone permission only for calls and video recording.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs or advertising ID. We do not show third-party ads and we do not sell your personal data. We do not store payment card details.

3. Why we use data

To create and secure your account, show your public profile and content to other users, deliver messages and notifications, run calls, provide the AI assistant, keep progress in sync between devices, review reports and enforce our Community Guidelines, and prevent abuse.

4. Who sees your data

  • Other users: your display name, username, bio, photo, banner, posts, Reels, followers/following counts and "last seen" are visible to signed-in users. Posts and Reels marked "Only me" are private. Chat messages are visible only to the participants (and everyone in the shared General chat).
  • Service providers (processors): Google Firebase (authentication, database, push notifications, AI Logic/Gemini, App Check/reCAPTCHA), Supabase (storage of Reels video files), Cloudflare (push-notification relay), Metered (TURN relay for calls), Google Fonts (font delivery).
  • Law enforcement / safety: we may disclose data when required by law or to protect users from serious harm.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings → Delete account. You can also request deletion without the app, see How to delete your account.

Deleting the account removes your profile, username, follows, blocks, posts, Reels, stories, your chat messages and files, support requests, "last seen", notification token and learning progress. The sign-in record is deleted from Firebase Authentication. Please note: video files of deleted Reels may remain in storage for a short period (up to 30 days) before they are permanently purged; likes and comments you left on other people's content may remain without a profile attached to them; reports that concern abuse may be kept as needed for safety and legal reasons.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are handled and hashed by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. Access to the database is protected by security rules.

7. Children

FION is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app, delete content you posted, block and report users, and delete your account. To request a copy of your data or any other privacy request, contact us using the details below. We reply within a reasonable time.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours (for example the United States and the European Union), with appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
