Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What we collect

When you register: your email, display name, username, optional bio and profile photo, and sign-in data (via email/password or Google). We also store your learning progress, follows and chat messages, and your subscription status. If you use the AI assistant, your questions are sent to an AI model to generate answers.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or store card details on this site. Payments are made by bank transfer arranged directly with us.

Who processes data

Google Firebase (authentication, database, hosting of app data), Google Gemini (AI answers).. Your device also stores settings and progress locally in your browser.

How we use it

To run your account, show your profile to other users, deliver chat, provide the AI assistant, manage your subscription and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

You can edit your profile in the app. To request access to or deletion of your data, contact us in Telegram (link below) and we will respond within a reasonable time.
